BUSINESS REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT (BRD)

1.1 Problem Statement
Right now, real estate investors, brokers, and property consultants in Abu Dhabi have a hard time checking if a rental property is actually a good deal. Most agencies rely on gut feeling, scattered spreadsheets, or incomplete property lists because they do not have a shared, data-backed system.
This manual approach causes a few major problems in everyday business operations: 

- **No Clear Standards**: It is hard to compare different neighbourhoods side-by-side to see where rent is actually fair compared to the physical size of the property.

-**Overpricing and Missed Opportunities**:Agents often market properties that are way overpriced without realizing it, or they miss out on great, underpriced deals that would give their clients an excellent return on investment (ROI).

**- Stagnant Listings:** Properties sit on the market for months without anyone tracking them. Because teams do not look at how old an ad is, companies waste their advertising budgets running ads for apartments that are either already taken or completely uncompetitive.

1.2 Strategic Business Objectives
The main goal of this project is to stop guessing and start using data to drive property decisions. By building this system, we want to help the platform achieve these specific goals:

**Make Better Recommendations:** Improve investment decisions by providing reliable rental market benchmarks, pricing insights, and performance indicators.

**Find the Best Areas:** Automatically rank neighbourhoods based on true price-to-size efficiency (Rent per Square Foot) rather than just looking at the total rent.

**Save Time:** Speed up the consultation process. Instead of taking days to manually research and compare properties for an investor, an advisory agent can cross-filter the parameters and deliver a reliable market benchmark  in seconds.

1.3 Target Operational Stakeholders
**Property Investors (HNI / NRI Clients)**: They need low-risk areas with proven high returns to invest their money safely.

**Real Estate Consultants:** They need instant proof of average market prices so they can negotiate better lease terms for their corporate clients.

**Brokerage Sales Teams**: They need real data and clear stories to convince leads to buy properties.
Property Management Firms: They need to find slow-moving listings early so they can fix marketing plans before properties become dead stock.

1.4 Core Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
**Rent per Sqft (Pricing Efficiency)**: This tracks exactly how much rent a property generates for each square foot of space. It is calculated by dividing total rent by the area size.
Listing Age (Market Velocity): This measures how many days a property ad has been live on the market since it was first posted.
**Demand Index:** This measures how fast a listing moves. A low average listing age means a high demand index, showing that properties in that area are highly desirable and selling fast.
